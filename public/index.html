Sim World guide

What this is

A tiny world on a grid. Little square creatures called agents live in it. Each agent gets hungry, thirsty and tired, and has to find berries, water and rest to stay alive. You watch from above and can change the rules of the world at any time with the dials. You are the god of this world.

The map

  • Grass: open ground the agents walk on.
  • Water: lakes. Agents can't walk on water, but they drink from the edge.
  • Trees: block movement. Berries like to grow beside them.
  • Berries: food. Eaten berries disappear and new ones grow over time.
  • Woman (circle) and man (square). Each agent has its own colour; children are drawn smaller and grow as they age. A dashed line shows where it is heading. Small marks: z asleep, ♥ just had a child, ⚔ fighting. Grey means dead; bodies disappear after a day. A white frame marks the agent you have selected.

Time

The world moves forward in small steps called ticks. Every tick, each agent's needs change a little, it decides what to do, and it moves one tile or performs one action. At normal speed there are 4 ticks per second, and a full day (240 ticks) takes about a minute. At night the map darkens and agents want to sleep.

The agent's needs

Each need is a number from 0 to 100, shown as a bar in the status panel.

  • Hunger rises every tick. Eating a berry lowers it.
  • Thirst rises every tick. Drinking next to a lake lowers it.
  • Energy falls every tick. Sleeping restores it.
  • Health drops whenever hunger or thirst hits 100 or energy hits 0, and slowly recovers otherwise. At 0 health the agent dies.

How the agent decides

Every tick the agent gives each of its five choices a score and does the one with the highest score:

  • eat = its hunger (if any berry can be reached)
  • seek_water = its thirst (if any water can be reached)
  • sleep = 100 − energy, plus a bonus at night
  • mate = just above wandering, but only when it is content and a partner is available (see below)
  • wander = a fixed score (currently )

So the agent wanders until a need climbs above the wander score, then deals with the biggest need first. To eat or drink it walks the shortest path to the nearest berry or lake edge, one tile per tick. Whatever it is already doing gets a small bonus so it doesn't keep changing its mind. You can see the live scores under “scores & recent actions” in the status panel; the winner is highlighted.

Life, family and fights

Agents are women (circles) and men (squares). They are born, grow up, have children, compete, fight, grow old and die.

  • Age and strength. Every agent has an age in in-world days and one strength number from 0 to 1. Children start at 0.25 and grow to full strength when they reach the Adult age. In the last Adult age days of their life, elders slowly drop to 0.5. Low strength means tiring faster and healing more slowly.
  • Lifespan. Each agent's natural lifespan is the Lifespan dial give or take 20%. Past it, they lose health every tick and soon die of old age.
  • Having children. A woman and a man can have a child when both are adults (not children or elders), both are content (hunger, thirst and tiredness all below the Wander base score), they are not family (parent and child, or brother and sister), she has not had a child within the Birth interval, and fewer than Max agents are alive. When that's true they walk to each other, and as soon as they stand side by side a baby is born next to the mother. Its sex is random.
  • Competing. Berries are first come, first served.
  • Fights. Two grown-ups of the same sex standing side by side may fight when at least one of them is hungry or thirsty. The chance each tick is the Aggression dial. The winner is random, but the stronger and healthier one usually wins. The loser loses health (Fight damage), and a fight can kill. Each agent fights at most once per in-world hour. Children never fight.
  • Death. Agents die of hunger, thirst, exhaustion, old age or a fight. The event log records births and deaths, with the cause and age.
  • New blood. A new world starts with two unrelated couples. If after a few generations everyone is related, no more children can be born and the group dies out, as small isolated groups can. Add agent brings in an unrelated newcomer.

What you can do

  • Dials: change any rule while the world runs. Changes apply on the next tick. Dials marked (on reset) only shape the next new world.
  • Click the map on empty grass to drop a berry.
  • Click an agent on the map, or its row in the status panel, to see its details (age, strength, parents, children, scores) and plot its history.
  • Add agent: bring in a grown-up newcomer of random sex. The ✕ in an agent's row removes it (dead ones too).
  • Reset world: start over with a fresh map from the chosen seed.

Saving, history and cleaning up

  • The world is saved automatically every tick. If the server stops, starting it again carries on exactly where it left off.
  • Snapshots are save points you make yourself. Load one to jump back to that moment. Anything that happened after it is discarded. Delete snapshots you no longer need with ✕.
  • The event log records every dial change and action with the tick it happened. Clear empties it.
  • The history chart shows an agent's needs over time. Clear history deletes the stored history but leaves the world as it is.
  • Everything is stored in one file, data/world.db, in the project folder. Deleting the data folder (with the server stopped) erases everything.

Things to try

  • Turn on Drought and watch berries become scarce.
  • Raise Hunger rate to 2 and see whether the agent can keep up.
  • Set Sleep bonus at night to 0 and the agent only sleeps when tired, day or night.
  • Save a snapshot, change something, then load it to compare what happens.
